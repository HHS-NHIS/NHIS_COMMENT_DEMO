Counts are database totals for the selected preliminary theme, or lexical matches when no theme is selected (conversational words such as “raise” or “worry” are ignored; multi-word concepts are searched as phrases). Neither represents verified numbers of unique people. A paid request sends your question plus up to ten excerpts — one per near-duplicate text group, with e-mail addresses and phone numbers removed — to OpenAI; it never sends the SQLite database. Each answer is automatically checked for citations to IDs that were not supplied, unsupported numbers, and quotes not found in the excerpts.
